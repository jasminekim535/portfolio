Migrant Compass

Migrants need a safe tool to be guided




Migrant Compass was born from the belief that migrants need a mobile platform that provides safety, guidance, and access to real-time resources. At the Idea Lab at Fletcher, our cross-functional team built the product from the ground up—transforming an initial concept into a fully realized solution.

Migrant Compass is an innovative mobile platform that supports migrants by providing real-time, location-specific data to enhance safety and informed decision-making during travel. It combines crowdsourced updates, official information, and predictive analytics to offer a comprehensive navigation tool tailored for vulnerable journeys.


TIME

September - November 2025
TEAM

Migrant Compass Team with Researchers, Engineers, and Developers

ROLE

UX Research & Design  

●USER NEED

Why Migrant Compass?
There is a clear need for a platform like Migrant Compass, providing migrants with a safe, accessible tool for real-time guidance, safety updates, and essential resources. Designed for offline and on-the-go use, it offers location-based information on food, shelter, and aid, along with updates on urgent local events and conditions. The app also enables users to share and access community information while prioritizing verified sources, safety, and user data protection.
  Logo Design

Target Users

The Migrant Compass is designed for migrants traveling alone or with families, offering critical navigation and safety information. It also supports humanitarian workers, local aid organizations, volunteers, and community centers by providing real-time data that enhances their ability to assist and protect vulnerable populations on the move.
Research & Design Methodologies

We utilized user personas, hierarchical task analysis, and wireframes to understand user needs and challenges. This comprehensive approach helped to ensure that the platform’s design remains user-centered throughout development.



Mood Board

The mood board showcase our initial visual direction for the project, combining color palettes, icon sets, typography, and layout inspirations. It reflects our goals for accessibility, clarity, and simplicity while maintaining a cohesive and modern design language that aligns with the platform’s purpose.


User Persona

Basic Profile
  • Age: 30
  • Status: Single mother of two (ages 5 & 8)
  • Background: From Puebla, fleeing violence and unemployment. Traveling through Mexico toward Tijuana.
  • Tech Use: Basic–intermediate; relies on WhatsApp, Facebook, and Google Maps. Android phone.
  • Work: Informal labor (domestic help, food sales).
  • Payment: Cash; family transfers via Oxxo/Western Union.

Biography

María is on a long, unstable journey with her children, moving between buses, on foot, and in temporary shelters. Her phone is her lifeline—used for navigation, communication, and migrant WhatsApp groups. She depends on NGOs and volunteers for safety and support. She hopes to reach the border and find legal protection and stability.

Core Needs

  • Real-time updates on safe shelters, checkpoints, aid
  • Offline maps + emergency contacts
  • Verified legal info on rights and asylum
  • Access to trusted NGOs/volunteers
  • Simple Spanish guidance, icons, and voice options

Frustrations

  • Misinformation on social media
  • Limited internet/charging
  • Fear of scams or corrupt authorities
  • Keeping her kids safe in unstable conditions
  • Feeling unseen in official processes

Personality

Protective · Resilient · Hopeful · Pragmatic

Quote

“Every day is uncertain, but I can’t stop — my kids depend on me.”

Task Analysis

We started the semester with a task analysis in Figma to lay out the app's structure and user interactions.

                           


           

Key Features

Key features include real-time risk alerts, AI-powered route-safety scoring, and offline access for areas with limited connectivity. These capabilities help migrants and humanitarian workers alike with essential tools to notify them of nearby risks so they can respond proactively, navigate efficiently, and provide access for everyone despite connectivity issues.


Final Product

We built our prototype in Figma and ensured we included everyone’s ideas on color schemes, layout, and accessibility options. 


●FUTURE IMPLICATIONS

Next Steps
We plan to further expand the platform by conducting deeper research into real-world locations, support services, and community resources to provide reliable, real-time data within the product. In the future, collaborating with nonprofit organizations could help validate and expand these resources while ensuring users have access to trusted support systems. By strengthening these partnerships and data sources, the platform has the potential to reduce user anxiety and uncertainty by up to 30% while improving overall trust and engagement.
