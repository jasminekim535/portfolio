COMPANY

During my internship as a UX & Graphic Design Intern at Kidod Science & Technologies Ltd., I redesigned the company’s pitch deck to better align with its brand identity and improve overall engagement. I collaborated closely with cross-functional team members to ensure stakeholder needs were clearly represented while maintaining a visually cohesive and easy-to-navigate design. Through multiple iterations, I refined the content structure by simplifying complex information, reducing excessive text, and enhancing readability. The final result was a polished, investor-ready pitch deck that effectively communicated key ideas with clarity and impact.
