Year 3147

Painting Mixed Media

TIME
March 2021
TOOLS
Acrylic paint, recycled plastic, dirt, plaster
CREDIT
Silver for Art in the Genius Olympiad Competition in 2021
Year 3147 is an approximate, yet distant, potential future that humans may face if continued pollution continues to ruin our environment. The plastic pollution that aquatic organisms suffer from is severe; due to the microplastics that float in the sea, many vital life forms that support the ecosystem are taken away. This is not a fact we are all unaware of; despite knowing this is a critical issue, we, as consumers and producers, abuse plastic for our mere convenience. I am a part of this, too; while brainstorming this art piece, I recognized the sheer amount of plastic we use daily, from cups to snack wrappings. The artwork highlights the indestructibility of plastic and its inability to degrade, resulting in an ever-growing accumulation of trash in our world. It achieves this by manufacturing realistic details of a fish using acrylic paint and replacing some of the fish's bones with precisely applied small pieces of plastic.
