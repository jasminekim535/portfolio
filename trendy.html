TRENDY

A cohesive app for beauty trends




Trendy was created to solve a common frustration among young women: keeping up with beauty and fashion trends often requires hours of searching across multiple platforms. Trendy simplifies this process by curating the latest products in one place, allowing users to discover trends, find inspiration, and shop seamlessly within a single app.

I built the product from the ground up—from concept and user research to interface design—focusing on a visually engaging, intuitive experience that makes discovering and shopping for trends fast, easy, and enjoyable.
