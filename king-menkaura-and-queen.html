King Menkaura and Queen

Museum of Fine Arts Artwork Wayfinding Design

This project explores wayfinding and exhibition design through a focused study of King Menkaura and Queen from the MFA’s Ancient Egypt: Old Kingdom collection. After visiting the museum and researching the artwork, I developed a cohesive visual system to guide visitors while enhancing the monumentality and mystique of the sculpture.

The project includes a directional panel, an MFA T Stop, a simplified exhibition map, promotional banners, and a series of posters. Design decisions were informed by the materiality and scale of the statue, using a deep teal palette inspired by greywacke stone, paired with semi-transparent gray and white typography. Emphasis on verticality, spatial hierarchy, and typographic scale reinforces the presence of King Menkaura while maintaining clarity across all touchpoints.

Through consistent color, typography, and layout, the final system creates a unified, immersive experience that balances functional navigation with visual storytelling.

●Research and Design

Wayfinding Design
After visiting the Museum of Fine Arts (MFA) and documenting several exhibitions through photography, I analyzed a range of artworks. I ultimately selected King Menkaura and Queen from the Ancient Egypt gallery as the focal point of my wayfinding project, conducting in-depth research and visual analysis of the sculpture.

I began by designing a directional panel that guides visitors toward the artwork while creating a sense of mystery and reverence. I also designed an MFA T Stop, an exhibition map that simplifies navigation by centering the artwork, and a large-scale banner to promote the Ancient Egypt: Old Kingdom exhibition within the museum.

Across all assets, I maintained a cohesive visual system through consistent typography, color, and layout. Design choices were inspired by the greywacke stone of the sculpture, using deep teal backgrounds with semi-transparent gray and white text. By emphasizing verticality, zooming in on the figures, and using scale in typography to highlight King Menkaura, the designs reinforce the monumentality of the statue while creating a unified, immersive experience.


●Final Design

Posters
I created a series of posters that extended the established color palette and typography inspired by the statue’s strong presence. Through intentional use of space, scale, and layout, each iteration explored clarity and visual impact. The left poster was selected as the final design and produced at a large scale, representing the strongest and most cohesive solution.
