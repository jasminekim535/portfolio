A wayfinding and exhibition system for King Menkaura and Queen, from the MFA’s Ancient Egypt: Old Kingdom collection, guiding visitors from the street to the statue.

Process

  1. 01ResearchVisit and photograph the MFA
  2. 02AnalyzeArtworks and the space
  3. 03WayfindingMap, signs and banner
  4. 04PostersExtend the identity

●Wayfinding

From the street to the gallery

A transit-stop sign, a map, a directional sign and an exterior banner share one dark teal palette and bold type, inspired by the statue’s strong presence.

●Final design

Posters

A poster series extends the palette and typography, letting the statue’s scale carry each composition.
