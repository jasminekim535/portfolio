Travel Mate

Improving the experience of planning travel with friends


I worked as a UX designer with a team with designers and developers to ideate and build Travel Mate, an app designed to enhance the trip-planning experience with friends. Through in-depth UX research, we identified a key problem: planning trips with friends can often be a time-consuming and exhausting process. By centralizing destination research for Korea and enabling real-time collaboration between travelers, the platform provides an all-in-one solution for organizing accommodations, tracking trip expenses, and discovering popular destinations—transforming a complex planning process into a streamlined, shared experience.

Over the course of development, we refined the interface design 16 times, continuously iterating based on user feedback and usability testing. My team and I collaborated to create 100+ detailed product screens, ultimately developing a fully functional product for both web and mobile platforms. Travel Mate simplifies trip planning, offering a seamless, enjoyable experience for users whether they’re exploring solo or coordinating with friends. Available in English and Korean.

●COMPARITIVE ADVANTAGE

Why Travel Mate?
While many travel tools offer individual features such as booking accommodations or discovering restaurants, they often require users to switch between multiple platforms and coordinate plans through endless back-and-forth communication with travel companions. This fragmented process makes collaborative trip planning time-consuming and stressful.

Travel Mate is a solution to this user need by enabling search for restaurants, hotels, and attractions, save locations, build routes and daily itineraries, and track shared expenses—all within a single app. By centralizing these tools, Travel Mate simplifies communication, reduces planning friction, and allows groups to easily adjust plans while keeping everything organized in one place.

●RESEARCH & PLANNING

Research & Benchmarking

Our team conducted comprehensive research using surveys, user data, and datasets from comparable apps, while also benchmarking their UI patterns and information architecture.

By distilling this information into precise needs and insights, we identified key opportunities and shaped a solution. This process allowed us to synthesize our findings and design a user-centered app that directly addresses real challenges.


●DESIGN FEATURES

Icons & Moodboards

The design team created a cohesive icon set to ensure visual consistency throughout the app.

We also benchmarked various platforms to examine their features and visual systems, and developed a moodboard outlining the color palette, aesthetics, and key functions.


●IA CREATION

I created the IA in collaboration with UI/UX designers, dividing the app into multiple screens and pages, including home, travel plan, recommended travel spots, and profile. We outlined the app’s core features and technicalities to ensure an optimized user experience and clearly highlight its key functionalities.

●FUTURE IMPLICATIONS

Next Steps
Future opportunities for Travel Mate include expanding partnerships and platform integrations to enhance functionality and create sustainable revenue streams. Potential developments include collaborating with partner platforms to place targeted advertisements, integrating with services such as mapping tools, restaurants, and hotel booking systems, and incorporating expense-splitting features for group travel.

Strategic partnerships and sponsored listings could allow businesses to promote their locations within the app, helping travelers discover relevant destinations at a discounted price while generating revenue. By further consolidating travel planning tools into a single ecosystem, Travel Mate could potentially reduce user friction by up to 30%, increase engagement, and create a scalable business model that benefits both users and partner organizations.
