At JumboHack, Tufts’ 48-hour hackathon, our team built HopOffer, a mobile app that helps college students find jobs through a swipe-based interface.

Challenge

Crowded applicant pools, and job platforms built for organizations rather than students.

Solution

A dating-app-inspired swipe UI with personalized recommendations, plus a recruiter side.

Result

A working MVP in React Native and Firebase, pitched to judges in 48 hours.
