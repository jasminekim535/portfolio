Hop Offer

Designing an app to improve the job application process


I participated in the Spring 2025 JumboHack, Tufts University's annual 48-hour hackathon. As undergraduates, we noticed that showcasing skills to recruiters was challenging due to oversaturated application pools and web platforms designed more for organizations than students. We created HopOffer to create a student-centric solution to streamline job discovery and enhance employment opportunities.

As the UI/UX designer, I designed an intuitive interface in Figma inspired by dating apps to maximize engagement and ease of use. I also collaborated closely with developers to implement the MVP using React Native and Firebase, gaining valuable hands-on experience in cross-platform development, real-time data integration, and the product-building process from end to end.

HopOffer is a mobile platform that simplifies the job search process for college students with a swipe-based UI, providing personalized job recommendations. HopOffer empowers students to navigate oversaturated job markets by streamlining discovery, application tracking, and recruiter visibility, bridging the gap between talent and opportunity with a modern, user-first approach.
