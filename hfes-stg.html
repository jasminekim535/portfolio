HFES STG 

Designing a website for the technical group


As Website Lead for the Human Factors Ergonomics Society – Sustainability Technical Group (STG), I led the end-to-end design of the organization’s website, from research to MVP and iteration.
I gathered insights from STG members through surveys and synthesized findings using affinity mapping to define user needs. I then conducted card sorting with 17 users to shape the site’s information architecture, collaborating with the team to finalize content structure and user flows. I translated these insights into low-fidelity wireframes and a functional MVP in Wix, followed by usability testing and interviews with 5 members. The feedback directly informed iterative design improvements and the development of a core user persona.

After the MVP creation, I expanded the site with additional sustainability resources and professional opportunities through further research. The website is now live, actively used within the organization, and continues to evolve through ongoing updates, design iterations, and content improvements.

●ABOUT

HFES STG
The Human Factors and Ergonomics Society (HFES) is the world’s largest scientific association for professionals in the human factors/ergonomics field, founded in 1957.

The Sustainable Technical Group (STG) is one of the many technical groups in the organization, a dynamic and collaborative forum that aims to promote the application of human factors and ergonomics principles to sustainability systems.



●PROBLEM- SOLVING

Need

↓

Solution
HFES STG has grown to 200+ members and produced extensive events, recordings, presentations, and resources. Despite this scale, STG lacked a centralized, easy-to-navigate website to unify leadership information, membership onboarding, event updates, and sustainability learning materials.

As website lead, the team and I developed an all-in-one digital resource for the STG that organizes members, archives content, supports event access, and serves as an entry point for new members to learn about sustainability practices and engage with the community.
●USER RESEARCH

Survey & Affinity Mapping
After sending out the survey to 200+ STG members, I identified key user priorities, including access to upcoming events, professional opportunities, sustainability best practices, past recordings, and centralized resources.

Users also expressed a strong interest in features like an event calendar and opportunities to connect with other members. In parallel, stakeholders emphasized the importance of highlighting past leadership and maintaining a comprehensive resource hub.

I synthesized these insights using affinity mapping to define core user needs and inform the overall direction of the website.




Card Sorting & IA/ Task Flow
I conducted card sorting with 17 users to inform the site’s information architecture, then collaborated with the team to finalize content structure and user flows.


●UX DESIGN

Low- fidelity Wireframes
Using insights from the research and planning phase, we created low-fidelity wireframes to organize page content and structure before moving into prototyping.


●PROTOTYPING

MVP
Using Wix, we developed an MVP prototype aligned with the brand’s color and typography, prioritizing core user needs to enable early launch and iterative testing.


●UT & INTERVIEWS

Usability Testing & Persona
Conducted interviews and usability testing with 5 STG members to identify improvement areas and better understand user needs and goals. With AI and human oversight, I developed a persona and informed iterative enhancements to the website.

While user interviews for persona creation are typically conducted earlier in the process, conducting them at this stage still provided valuable insights to inform ongoing design decisions.



●LAUNCH

Improving Website & Additional Resources
After usability testing, I identified key improvements that could be made to the MVP and translated feedback into iterative design and structural improvements to enhance overall user experience.

Based on further research and user needs, we expanded the content to include sustainability and professional resources, creating a more useful, accessible, and well-organized platform for the target audience.

The website launched and became an ongoing resource for STG members and anyone interested in sustainability and human factors. The website team members will continue to update information and improve the design moving forward.
