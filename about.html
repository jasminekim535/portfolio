My Design Philosophy


                                 
               01  
Creativity- I find that creativity is crucial in UI/UX design to brainstorm new and innovative solutions and design. Constantly being inspired by my surroundings and challenging the norm is what leads to creative designs.

               02                            
Problem Solving- Seeking ways to improve things and finding solutions to problems is what drives advancement. Being able to analyze and find problems through user research and interviews is essential in UI/UX design.
         
               03
Curious & Compassionate- Caring about people and creating an improved experience for them is the essence of UI/UX design, and being aware of their struggles and empathizing with them, is essential.
