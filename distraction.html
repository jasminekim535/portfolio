Distraction

Poster Design with the Concept of  Social Media Addiction

TIME
December 2023
TOOLS
Graphic Design Posters
CREDIT
Instructed by Mary Banas
SMFA 2023 Fall
Focusing on the topic of social media addiction, a prevalent issue in today’s society, I created a compelling series of poster designs to explore and emulate this concept. The series reflects the overwhelming and often chaotic nature of social media consumption, incorporating vibrant colors and intricate, overstimulating designs to mirror the sensory overload we experience daily. These visuals symbolize the dopamine-driven cycle perpetuated by the constant use of social media platforms. By leveraging cutting-edge technology and intentionally addictive features, these apps are designed to capture and retain our attention, fostering habits that can often border on compulsion. My experimentation with this series not only highlights the psychological impact of social media addiction but also invites viewers to reflect on their own relationships with technology and the increasingly blurred boundaries between the virtual and real worlds.
