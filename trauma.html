Trauma.

Painting Mixed Media

TIME
August 2022
TOOLS
Acrylic paint, oil pastel, charcoal, tape
CREDIT
Self-experiment
The concept of trauma was explored to demonstrate the impact on identities, and mine as well. Time passes, and we grow, but sometimes we hold on to past traumas, which is reflected in the imagery of a figure trapped in a box and in a compacted position. Overwhelmingly adverse events are challenging to move past and significantly impact my identity. The struggles created by trauma are shown through the gruesome imagery of the decapitated fish heads and tails in the background and the gross green colors. Intense colors and rough lines used to depict fish were inspired by the work of Jean-Michel Basquiat. Experimenting with different positions that showed pain, I drew the man with his hands on his head with charcoal to create contrast with the background and add details. The posture even looks like a skull from afar, demonstrating the death-like feeling felt from trauma.
