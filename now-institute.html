The client

What is the Now Institute?

The NOW Institute is Morphosis Architects’ urban research wing, led by Thom Mayne and Eui-Sung Yi. It develops planning and recovery strategies with cities from Haiti to Los Angeles.

As an intern, I designed two separate websites for it.

01Noto Nownotonow.orgA new bilingual site for its Noto Peninsula recovery research. 02Now Institutenowinstitute.orgA redesign of the institute’s own website.
Challenge

Outdated content, broken pages and two disconnected sites hid the institute’s work.

Solution

One rebuilt, responsive site for the institute, and a separate bilingual site for Noto Now.

Result

Two live sites with a simple shared design system, launched in three months.

Process: designing and vibe coding

I designed in Figma, then built both sites myself by vibe coding with Claude Code, which read my Figma files through MCP.

  1. 01BenchmarkSimilar institutions, Figma Make
  2. 02DesignFigma layouts & design system
  3. 03BuildClaude Code + Figma MCP
  4. 04DeployGitHub & Vercel
  5. 05IterateLayout, type, copy, content

Design in the real medium

I tuned type, spacing and interactions on live pages, not static mockups.

Faster loops with stakeholders

Every change went live on Vercel as a shareable link for feedback.

From designer to builder

Owning code and deploys let me ship two sites in three months.

Key takeaways

Working in an architecture office taught me as much about people as about interfaces.

01

Balance stakeholder needs

Leadership, partners and the public wanted different things. I learned to weigh them in one design.

02

Present, listen, iterate

I presented my work often and turned feedback into concrete changes.

03

Coordinate to get what you need

I delegated requests and followed up to collect the content and approvals I needed.

04

Research before design

Studying every project first made the content accurate and the structure clear.

05

Narrative is part of the UX

Content order shapes understanding: a story for Noto Now, an archive for the institute.

06

Vibe coding makes designers builders

Claude Code let me test ideas in the real medium and ship two sites myself.
