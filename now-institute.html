At Morphosis Architects I designed and launched two websites: the Now Institute, an urban research initiative, and Noto Now, a bilingual site for the Noto Peninsula’s recovery after the 2024 earthquake.

Challenge

Outdated content, broken pages and two disconnected sites hid the institute’s work.

Solution

One rebuilt, responsive site for the institute, and a separate bilingual site for Noto Now.

Result

Two live sites with a simple shared design system, launched in three months.
