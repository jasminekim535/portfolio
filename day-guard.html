As a Graphic Design Intern at a health and wellness startup, I worked across teams to create high-converting visual assets for Instagram, Meta ads, and email marketing campaigns. I developed on-brand graphics that aligned with the company’s aesthetic while supporting performance-driven growth goals.

Collaborating directly with cross-functional team members and founders, I translated complex product benefits into engaging, educational, and visually distinct creative concepts tailored to different campaign objectives. I also integrated AI tools into my design process to accelerate ideation, generate creative variations, and refine messaging, enabling faster iteration and more optimized campaign assets.

This role strengthened my ability to combine strategic thinking, creative storytelling, and data-informed design to drive engagement and business impact.




●Meta-ads

Amazon Reviews Creative
Using insights from Amazon customer reviews, I designed a series of Meta ad creatives highlighting key positive features of the jelly stick. I curated impactful customer quotes and crafted eye-catching copy that translated real user feedback into compelling visual narratives.

I developed two distinct design concepts, each with unique imagery and layouts, and adapted them into three ad formats with varying aspect ratios to optimize performance across multiple Meta placements. This project demonstrates my ability to leverage user insights, create conversion-focused designs, and execute scalable ad systems for digital marketing.
