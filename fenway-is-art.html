Fenway Is Art

Fenway Postcard Design 



While researching a specific location, Fenway, I discovered that it is a vibrant hub brimming with art and culture. From its public installations and murals to the architecture and natural landscapes, art seems to infuse every corner of this iconic neighborhood. To capture the essence of Fenway, I immersed myself in its environment, taking photographs to document its unique artistic elements. I also delved into its rich history by reading articles and uncovering stories about its cultural significance. Using these insights, I designed a postcard and presentation to celebrate Fenway’s artistic spirit and to inspire others to see art in everything around them. This project is a call to action, encouraging people to explore, observe, and appreciate the often-overlooked beauty in their surroundings, thereby fostering a deeper connection to the spaces they inhabit.
