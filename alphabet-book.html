The Alphabet Book

Book design with experimentation of text

TIME
December 2023
TOOLS
Book Design
CREDIT
Instructed by Mary Banas
SMFA 2023 Fall
As part of my exploration into the world of typography, I undertook the creation of a comprehensive book that served as a series of experimental studies. This project encompassed a wide range of design elements, including typeface development, texture exploration, pattern creation, and poster design, all centered around the integration of text. Starting from scratch, I designed my own type and imagery, which allowed me to delve deeply into the nuances of typography and its interaction with visual elements. Through this process, I developed a more profound understanding of the fundamental principles of shapes, forms, and their role in effective communication. This hands-on approach not only enhanced my technical skills but also deepened my appreciation for the intricate relationship between design and storytelling.





●Experimenting with Typography

Creating My Own Type
Manipulating fonts and shapes, I made a series of my own unique fonts that do not resemble letters. Working both online and on paper, I gained hands-on experience dealing with shapes and forms, developing a deeper understanding of the basics. Furthermore, I added names of the new fonts based on the vibe of the imagery.  


●Patterns and Textures

Creating Patterns With Type
With a mixture of types I made and found ones existing, I created patterns and shapes defying what is concieved as typography. Exploring the various textures and imagery that fonts can create, I gained a deeper understanding of overlapping and negative space, as well as the unique characteristics of each font.


●Thinking Outside of the Box

Creating Imagery With Type
Observing objects around me, I manipulated shapes into type. Experimenting with foreign objects and changing the form of existing type helped me think more creatively. I was able to look at the form and textures of various “types” and create pictorial compositions with uniqueness and depth.



●Making imagery

Poster Designs  
I created a series of posters experimenting with textures, lines, shapes, and text.
